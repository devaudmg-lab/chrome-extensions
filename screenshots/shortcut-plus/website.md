# Shortcut+

**Tagline:** Unlimited, organized shortcuts on your New Tab page.

## What it does
Shortcut+ replaces Chrome's New Tab page with a clean page of shortcuts that has no 10-tile limit. Sort your sites into groups, give tiles an emoji or a colour, place them anywhere, and open them with a click, a number key or a few typed letters. Everything stays on your device.

## Key features
- **Unlimited shortcuts** with automatic icons, emoji or uploaded icons, and tile colours
- **Groups** you can collapse, rename, reorder, and open all at once (tabs, new window or incognito)
- **Type to open**: type part of a name in the search box and press Enter to open the shortcut
- **Number keys**: press 1–9 to open your first nine shortcuts
- **Undo** for deleted shortcuts and groups, and Ctrl+Z for layout changes
- **Import from Chrome bookmarks**: turn a bookmarks folder into a group of shortcuts
- **Free placement** with smart guides, align and distribute tools, and resizing, or a tidy smart grid
- **Profiles** (up to 8), e.g. Work and Personal, each with its own shortcuts and look
- **Wallpapers**: built-in images and animated scenes, or your own image, GIF or video
- Optional widgets (clock, weather, focus timer, to-do, notes) and a small offline arcade
- Every open New Tab page stays in sync
- Backup and restore to a file

## How to use
1. Install Shortcut+ and open a new tab.
2. Press **N** (or the + button) to add a shortcut. Leave the name empty and it is filled in from the address.
3. Press **G** to make a group, or drag tiles onto a group.
4. Type a few letters of a shortcut's name in the search box and press **Enter** to open it, or press **1–9**.
5. Press **E** to edit the layout. Switch to **Free Placement** to drag tiles anywhere.
6. To bring in bookmarks, open **Settings → Import from Chrome bookmarks** and pick a folder.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- **Ctrl+K** or **/**: focus the search box
- Type a name, then **Enter**: open the matching shortcut (**↓** then Enter searches the web instead)
- **1 … 9**: open the 1st to 9th shortcut (**Alt** + number: incognito)
- **Ctrl+Click** / **Ctrl+Shift+Click** / **Alt+Click**: open in a new tab / new window / incognito
- **N** new shortcut, **G** new group, **E** edit layout, **M** multi-select, **S** settings, **R** arcade
- **Arrow keys** and **Enter**: move between tiles and open one
- **Ctrl+Z** / **Ctrl+Y**: undo / redo layout changes
- **Esc**: close panels, clear the selection, leave edit mode

## Permissions, explained simply
- **Replace your New Tab page**: that is where your shortcuts appear.
- **Storage** and **unlimited storage**: your shortcuts, settings and any background image or video you upload are saved on your computer.
- **Read your bookmarks** (optional): asked only when you click *Import from Chrome bookmarks*. Bookmarks are read on your device, never sent anywhere, and the access is given back right after the import.
- **suggestqueries.google.com** (optional): only if you turn on Google search suggestions; then what you type in the search box is sent to Google for suggestions.

## FAQ
**Does Shortcut+ collect my data?**
No. There is no account and no server. Shortcuts, settings and history stay in Chrome on your computer.

**Pressing Enter opened a shortcut, but I wanted to search.**
When your text matches the start of a shortcut's name, that shortcut is selected first. Press **↓** once to choose "Search Google", or add more words to your search.

**Why does Chrome ask about my bookmarks?**
Only the bookmark import needs it, so Shortcut+ asks at that moment and releases the access afterwards. You can say no and keep using everything else.

**Can I undo a delete?**
Yes. Click **Undo** in the message that appears after deleting a shortcut, a selection or a group.

**Do my shortcuts sync between computers?**
Not automatically. Use **Settings → Export** and **Import** to move them.

**Can I open shortcuts in incognito?**
Yes, with Alt+Click, once you switch on "Allow in Incognito" for Shortcut+ in chrome://extensions.

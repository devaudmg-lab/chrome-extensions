# WebDrawPro

**Tagline:** Draw, number, blur and measure on any web page, then share it in one click.

## What it does
WebDrawPro adds a small floating toolbar to any web page so you can mark it up: arrows, boxes, circles, highlights, text and numbered steps. Blur private details before you share, measure things in pixels, then copy or save a screenshot with your notes on it. Drawings stay attached to the page as you scroll.

## Key features
- **Pen, highlighter, rectangle, circle, arrow and text** tools with colours, widths and fill
- **Numbered steps** for how-to guides and bug reports
- **Blur or black out** emails, keys and faces before sharing
- **Ruler** that shows distance and angle in pixels
- **Select, move, resize and recolour** any shape; double-click a note to edit it
- **Drawings saved per page** on your computer, so they are still there next time
- **Undo and redo** everything, including Clear All
- **Copy to clipboard** or **save as PNG** with your drawings
- Works with a **mouse, pen tablet or touch screen**
- Drawings **scroll with the page**, and stay visible while the toolbar is minimized

## How to use
1. Install WebDrawPro and pin it to your toolbar.
2. Open any web page and click the icon (or press **Alt+Shift+D**).
3. Pick a tool and draw. Hold **Shift** for squares and straight 45° lines. Press **N** and click to add numbered steps.
4. Press **B** and drag over anything private to blur it. Turn on **Fill** first for a solid black box.
5. Click **Copy** to paste the result into chat or an issue, or **Save** to download a PNG.
6. Click the orange button (or press **Alt+Shift+D**) to minimize the toolbar. Your drawings stay on the page and you can use it normally.
7. Come back later and open WebDrawPro on the same page: your drawings are restored.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- **Alt+Shift+D**: open / minimize the toolbar
- **V** select, **P** pen, **H** highlighter, **R** rectangle, **O** circle, **A** arrow, **T** text
- **N** numbered step, **B** blur, **M** ruler, **E** eraser, **?** help
- **Shift** while drawing: square / 45° lines
- **Ctrl+Z** undo, **Ctrl+Y** or **Ctrl+Shift+Z** redo
- **Delete** removes the selected shape, **Esc** deselects
- Shortcuts never fire while you are typing in the page

## Permissions, explained simply
- **Active tab**: WebDrawPro only works on the tab where you click it, and only after you click. It uses this to add the toolbar and take the screenshot for Save and Copy.
- **Scripting**: puts the drawing toolbar on that page.
- **Storage**: remembers your colour and pen size, and keeps your drawings for each page, only on your computer. Nothing is saved in incognito windows.

WebDrawPro cannot read your other tabs, has no server and sends nothing anywhere.

## FAQ
**Is the blur safe for sensitive information?**
The blur is applied to the screenshot you save or copy, so the original text is not readable in the image. For very sensitive data, check the image before sharing, and turn on Fill before using the blur tool to get a solid black box.

**Do my drawings stay after I reload the page?**
Yes. They are saved for that page address on your computer. Open WebDrawPro on the page again and they come back. Use the bin button to clear a page, or "Forget all pages" in the help panel (?) to delete everything. Nothing is saved in incognito.

**Can the website see my drawings or notes?**
No. The toolbar and drawings sit in a sealed-off part of the page that the site's scripts cannot read, and the screenshot never touches the page.

**Why does Copy sometimes say it is blocked?**
A few websites do not allow clipboard access. Use Save instead, which always works.

# Smooth Zoom

**Tagline:** Zoom into any web page exactly where you point.

## What it does
Smooth Zoom lets you zoom toward your mouse pointer on any page, like zooming a map. Hold Alt and scroll to get a closer look at small text, charts and photos without changing the page layout. A magnifier loupe and one-click image fitting make details easy to read.

## Key features
- **Zoom toward the cursor** with Alt + scroll, up to 1000%, with text that stays sharp
- **Magnifier loupe** that follows your mouse, with adjustable power and size
- **Alt + click an image** to make it fill the window
- **Hand tool**: press H, or hold Space, and drag to move around the zoomed page, just like in Photoshop
- **Fixed headers and pop-ups stay in view** when you zoom on a scrolled page
- **Pan** with the arrow keys, the middle mouse button or Shift + drag
- **Turn it off for one site** when that site uses Alt + scroll itself
- Keyboard zoom, a zoom badge, and a quick reset with Esc
- Light and dark popup, no data collection

## How to use
1. Install Smooth Zoom and reload any tabs that were already open.
2. Point at something small, hold **Alt** and scroll up to zoom in.
3. Press **H** for the hand tool, or just hold **Space**, and drag to look around (or use the arrow keys). Press H or Esc to put the hand down.
4. Hold **Alt** and click an image or chart to fit it to the window.
5. Press **Alt+Shift+M** for the magnifier loupe. Hold Alt and scroll to change its power, or Alt + Shift and scroll to change its size.
6. Press **Esc** to go back to normal.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- **Alt + scroll**: zoom toward the mouse
- **Alt + Plus / Alt + Minus**: zoom in / out
- **Alt + click** on an image: fit it to the window
- **Arrow keys** (Shift for bigger steps): pan while zoomed
- **H**: hand tool on/off while zoomed (drag to move, Esc exits)
- **Hold Space**: hand tool while held (only while zoomed)
- **Alt + Shift + scroll**: loupe size
- **Middle-drag** or **Shift + drag**: pan while zoomed
- **Alt+Shift+M**: magnifier loupe
- **Alt + 0** or **Esc**: reset / close the loupe

## Permissions, explained simply
- **Read and change data on websites**: needed to notice Alt + scroll and to zoom the page. Nothing is collected or sent.
- **Storage**: remembers your settings, such as the maximum zoom, loupe size and sites where zoom is off.
- **Active tab**: only after you press the loupe shortcut or open the popup, so the loupe can magnify a picture of the visible page. That picture is never saved or sent anywhere.

## FAQ
**Does it change the page layout or text size?**
No. It magnifies the page like a camera zoom, so nothing re-flows or jumps around.

**A site already uses Alt + scroll. What can I do?**
Open the popup on that site and untick "Enabled on this site". Zoom keeps working everywhere else.

**Why does the loupe disappear for a moment when I scroll?**
The loupe magnifies a snapshot of the visible page. After you scroll it takes a fresh snapshot, which takes a fraction of a second.

**Will holding Space stop my videos from pausing, or stop Space from scrolling?**
No. Space only works as a hand while the page is zoomed in. At 100% it scrolls as usual, and on pages with a video it is always left to the player. You can also turn it off in the popup.

**Why is zoomed text sharp now?**
Since version 1.5 the browser redraws the page at the zoom level instead of enlarging a picture of it, so small text and thin chart lines stay crisp.
